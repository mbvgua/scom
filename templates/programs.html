Our Programs

We provide holistic, faith-centered programs designed to support the physical, educational, social, and spiritual development of children living with disabilities. Many of our children come from low-income families and informal settlements facing extreme economic strain, which often compromises their healthcare, nourishment, and learning opportunities.

Through our full-boarding facility, specialized care staff, therapy professionals, and feeding initiatives, SCOM delivers individualized support tailored to each child's unique developmental needs, fostering dignity, mobility, and long-term independence.
