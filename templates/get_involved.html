Get Involved

Your support matters because it directly restores health, dignity, and a bright future to children who would otherwise be hidden away or forgotten. Rooted in Scripture—where Jesus calls us to serve "the least of these" (Matthew 25:40)—we invite you to partner with us through financial donations, gifts of food, diapers, and mobility aids, or by contributing toward our €98,000 (KES 15,000,000) building project to construct a permanent home for 100 children.

Whether you volunteer your time, share your resources, or keep our home in your prayers, your partnership ensures every child experiences love, grows in confidence, and fulfills their God-given potential.

Volunteering
