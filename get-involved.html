Guide A Future

Mentoring

As a mentor, you offer more than just your time—you provide a trusted sounding board, encouragement, and real-world guidance to help a child navigate life’s opportunities.

Our mentoring program pairs dedicated adults with youth to build confidence, support academic and career goals, and foster long-term emotional resilience.

Frequently Asked Questions
Mentors typically commit 2 to 4 hours per month for a minimum of 1 year to build a meaningful, consistent bond.
Yes! All prospective mentors complete a background screening and a brief orientation program before being paired.
We match based on shared interests, career aspirations, personality traits, and geographic location for best compatibility.
We offer both in-person and hybrid virtual mentoring options, depending on your availability and the youth's age group.
